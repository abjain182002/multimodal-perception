## What progress claims mean

The 16-frame pilot proves the **CLOCs data pipeline produces real, auditable training labels**. It does not establish model learning, KITTI fused AP, cross-dataset transfer or completion of the whole project. Neither the past “80% of Phase 1” estimate nor a replacement “one-third” estimate can be substantiated from the experiment evidence and must not be used as a scientific progress metric. As of 26 September, the principal trained-fusion comparison and several locked-scope methods remain open. GPU limits, missing Azitt weights and missing nuScenes data place the 8–10 October technical target at material risk; surface blockers to the supervisor promptly if unresolved.
